# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed audience: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating fit, peers and collaborators discovering publications and projects, and readers following blog writing. The same content serves all three — experience, writing, and public work samples.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Siddhartha Chandra showcasing 15+ years across backend engineering, data science, AI, teaching, and startup building. Success looks like a visitor finishing with a clear, credible picture of breadth and depth — and a reason to reach out.

## Positioning

A thoughtful technologist with broad impact — engineering, data, teaching, and building, held together by a bias toward useful outcomes.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: LinkedIn / GitHub (professional connection, portfolio viewing)
- Secondary CTA: Email (direct contact)
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Systems, data, and people — that's the range."
- Belief ladder: This person has deep, broad expertise → this person ships real, visible work → this person communicates thoughtfully → worth connecting with.
- Proof on hand: Resume lists concrete outcomes (10X speed gain, 1.3M€ fund, 5+ papers). Blog shows writing quality. Publication links establish research credibility.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, calm, capable. Voice is direct and substantive — no fluff, no hype. The feel is someone who has done real work across enough domains to see the patterns.

## Anti-references

Over-designed or flashy surfaces. No gradient text, no glassmorphism, no decorative grid backgrounds. The site earns attention through content, not chrome.

## Design Principles

1. Substance over flash: every element earns its place. Decoration that doesn't communicate is removed.
2. Breadth without clutter: show the range across four domains without overwhelming the visitor on any single screen.
3. Calm confidence: the design signals capability through precision and restraint, not volume.
4. One site, one voice: the same direct, grounded tone across home, resume, and blog — no register shift between pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practices: 4.5:1 body contrast, semantic HTML, responsive layout, reduced-motion-friendly.
