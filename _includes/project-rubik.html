Reinforcement learning & calibrated decision models

A Rubik's cube solver that treats move selection as calibrated decisions. Steers 4 classic algorithms (Beginner, CFOP, Kociemba, Optimal BFS) with open RLCD models (TypeSafe Jev & Convai Laya), supporting custom face palettes, real-time PostgreSQL/SQLite graph synchronization, and an interactive 3D hemisphere state transition manifold.
